NEXUS WORKPLACE SERVICES
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT GUIDE
Document ID: FIN-EXP-006 | Version: 1.4 | Effective date: 1 October 2026

1. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Reasonable business expenses may be reimbursed when they are necessary for an approved business activity and supported by appropriate evidence.
Examples include approved local transport, business-related supplies and approved accommodation.

2. NON-ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Personal shopping, fines, personal entertainment, alcohol and expenses for family members are not reimbursable.
An expense is not automatically eligible simply because a receipt is available.

3. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
Employees should submit claims within 10 working days after the expense or trip, as applicable.
Late claims require an explanation and approval from the Finance Manager.

4. RECEIPTS
Receipts are mandatory for individual expenses above ₦10,000.
For expenses of ₦10,000 or below, employees should still provide a transaction record where available.
A receipt must show the supplier, date and amount.

5. APPROVAL
The employee's line manager reviews the business purpose and confirms that the expense was authorized.
Finance checks supporting documents and policy compliance before processing payment.
The Finance Manager approves exceptions to this guide.

6. PROCESSING
Complete claims are normally processed within seven business days after Finance approval.
Incomplete claims may be returned for clarification.
