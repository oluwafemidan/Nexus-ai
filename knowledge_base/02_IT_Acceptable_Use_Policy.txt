NEXUS WORKPLACE SERVICES
IT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Document ID: IT-SEC-002 | Version: 3.0 | Effective date: 15 August 2026

1. ACCOUNT SECURITY
Employees must use unique passwords for company accounts. Passwords must not be shared with colleagues, supervisors or external parties.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all company email and business-critical accounts where supported.
Employees must report suspected account compromise to the IT Service Desk immediately.

2. DEVICES
Company devices must use supported operating systems and receive security updates.
Employees must not disable endpoint protection, firewall controls or device encryption without written authorization from IT Security.
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately, even if the device is believed to be powered off.

3. DATA HANDLING
Confidential company information must be stored only in approved company-managed services.
Employees must not upload confidential documents to public AI tools unless the tool has been approved for that data classification.
Files containing customer or employee personal information must be shared only with authorized recipients.

4. SOFTWARE
Software must be obtained from approved sources.
Employees must not install unlicensed software or tools that bypass company security controls.
Requests for new software should be submitted through the IT Service Desk.

5. REMOTE WORK
Employees should use the company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems from untrusted networks.
Public Wi-Fi should not be used for sensitive work unless protected by the approved VPN.
Screens must be locked when devices are unattended.

6. INCIDENT REPORTING
Report suspicious emails using the email client's phishing-report function or forward them to security@nexus.example.
Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
