NEXUS WORKPLACE SERVICES
STAFF TRAVEL POLICY
Document ID: HR-TRV-004 | Version: 2.1 | Effective date: 1 September 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how employees request approval for business travel and claim eligible travel expenses.

2. APPROVAL
All domestic business travel must be approved by the employee's line manager before a ticket or accommodation is booked.
International travel requires approval from both the line manager and the Operations Director.
A travel request should state the business purpose, destination, proposed dates and estimated cost.

3. BOOKING
Employees should use the company's approved travel provider where available.
Economy class is the standard for domestic flights. Any exception requires written approval from the Operations Director.
Employees should choose reasonably priced accommodation within a practical distance of the work location.

4. CLAIMS
Expense claims must be submitted within 10 working days after the trip ends.
Receipts are required for individual expenses above ₦10,000.
Claims must include the trip purpose, date, expense category and amount.
Personal entertainment and expenses for accompanying family members are not reimbursable.

5. EMERGENCIES
If urgent travel is required and the line manager cannot be reached, the employee should contact the Operations Director. The employee must document the circumstances and obtain retrospective written approval as soon as practical.

6. CONTACT
Policy owner: Human Resources.
For interpretation questions, contact hr@nexus.example.
